    About this channel

    A modern, unofficial remake of the Wii Forecast Channel (2006–2013). It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo. Everything here uses free, public data, mostly from U.S. government science agencies.

    Where the data comes from

    • Forecast numbers: NOAA Global Forecast System (GFS) and HRRR models, run by the U.S. National Weather Service. They're served as JSON by Open-Meteo, which passes on the raw model output with no editing. Every location on Earth uses this same model.
    • Official U.S. forecasts & alerts: National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov).
    • Globe imagery: NASA Blue Marble: Next Generation, VIIRS Black Marble night lights and GPM IMERG precipitation, streamed from NASA GIBS. The original channel used Blue Marble too.
    • Pollen: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (European Union). It covers Europe only.
    • UV scale: World Health Organization / U.S. EPA UV Index categories.
    • Place search: GeoNames gazetteer (CC BY 4.0).
    • Sun & stars: computed in your browser from the current time.

    Notes

    The laundry check is calculated on this page from forecast humidity, wind, sunshine and rain chance. The music is an original loop synthesized live in your browser.

    Tip: use the ← and → keys to flip between screens, just like the arrows on the original.
